# PRODUCT.md

## Product Purpose

bitsocialforge.com is the company website for Bitsocial Forge Inc., the
infrastructure company accelerating peer-to-peer social media on the open
Bitsocial protocol. The site exists to make one thing legible: Forge builds
convenience infrastructure without owning the protocol or its users. Its first
products are Forge RPC, an in-development non-custodial Bitsocial RPC, and
Forge Images, an upcoming free media host for Bitsocial clients.

brand

## Users

- Bitsocial community members and app users (5chan, Seedit) checking who is
  behind the infrastructure.
- Developers and node operators evaluating Forge RPC versus self-hosting.
- Bitsocial client users checking how Forge Images handles access, durability,
  moderation, and legal-abuse procedures before it launches.
- Crypto-native builders and potential partners/investors who read whitepapers
  and master plans for fun.
- They are terminal-literate, skeptical of platform lock-in, allergic to
  corporate SaaS aesthetics and custody grabs.

## Brand and Tone

- Voice: plain, technical, confident. Claims are verifiable ("anchor key
  returned once, never stored"), never hype ("revolutionary", "web3 future").
- The company is the blacksmith, not the landlord: it forges tools for the
  network, it does not own the network.
- Terminal culture: monospace, prompts, protocol-speak. The site should feel
  like a machine built by people who live in terminals.
- Key phrases: "Accelerate peer-to-peer social media", "convenience
  infrastructure, not the owner of the protocol", "earned, not enforced",
  "no servers, no global bans", "keys over custody".

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: purple gradients, floating 3D blobs, hero
  metrics, cookie-cutter card grids, "Trusted by" logo walls.
- Crypto-hype sites: neon glassmorphism, token-price widgets, rocket emojis.
- Anything that implies custody, platform ownership, or "sign up to access".

## Strategic Principles

1. Truth over polish: never present in-development products (Forge RPC and
   Forge Images) as live; never invent roadmap items beyond published plans.
2. The open protocol comes first: always distinguish Bitsocial (open protocol,
   bitsocial.net) from Bitsocial Forge Inc. (company).
3. Exit is the product: the non-custodial and exit guarantees are the core
   differentiator and should stay above the fold of any product section.
4. Static craft: the site itself demonstrates the ethos — a small Vite-built
   React/TypeScript app, fast, self-hosted, and no trackers beyond explicitly
   approved deployment telemetry.
5. Forge Images is free by design: no paid hosting tiers, subscriptions, or
   per-upload fees. Voluntary support never changes access, limits, retention,
   moderation, or availability, and Forge RPC payments confer no Images benefit.
